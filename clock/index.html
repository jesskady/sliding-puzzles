How the clock puzzle works

The puzzle has two faces with 9 clocks each. It's solved when all 18 clocks point to 12 (the orange mark).

The parts

  • Wheels sit on the four corners. Drag around a wheel to turn it. Clockwise turns the clocks clockwise as seen from the side you're dragging on.
  • Pins sit between the clocks. Each pin sticks out on one side and is pushed in on the other. Tap a pin to push it through. Yellow means it sticks out toward you.
  • Corner clocks are shared. Each corner clock is geared to the matching corner on the back. When one turns, the other turns the opposite way. That's why the back's corners always mirror the front's.

What a wheel moves

  1. Look at the pin next to the wheel you're turning.
  2. If that pin sticks out toward you, every pin that sticks out on your side locks its 2×2 block of clocks to the wheel. All those clocks turn together.
  3. If that pin is pushed in, the turn happens on the back instead, using the pins that stick out back there.
  4. The back's corners always come along for the ride, turning the opposite way.

Touch and hold a wheel to see which clocks it will move. They light up blue on both faces.

Things to try

  • Push all four pins out toward you and turn any wheel: the whole face turns at once, and the back's corners spin the other way.
  • Push out just one pin: only that 2×2 block turns.
  • Push out two pins side by side: a 2×3 block turns. The center and edge clocks between them always move together.
  • Corners are easy because each one has its own wheel. The edges and center are what take planning.
