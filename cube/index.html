How the swipes work

  • Drag any sticker. Its row or column turns the way your finger goes.
  • Front face: right column up/down is R/R', left column down/up is L/L', top row left/right is U/U', bottom row right/left is D/D'.
  • The ring hugging the front face (the inner row of each surrounding panel) is F. Drag it clockwise for F, counterclockwise for F'.
  • 4×4 and 5×5: an outer row always turns alone. The SLICE / WIDE block decides what an inner row does. On SLICE it turns just that slice (2R). On WIDE it turns everything from that slice out to the face (Rw). Tap the block to switch.
  • Swipe the cross-arrows pad to turn the whole cube: left/right is y, up/down is x. Swipe the curved-arrows pad right for z (clockwise) and left for z'.
  • 3×3 only: the middle row or column can also turn the whole cube (see the setting above).
  • The back panel is an x-ray view through the cube from the front, so its top-right sticker sits directly behind the front's top-right. The outer row of each side panel and the back panel give you B moves without rotating.
  • Each finger counts separately, so two thumbs can turn at once. The timer starts on your first turn after a scramble; cube rotations don't start it.
